# D1: ports sell iron and salt; demand from stocks (D121)

## What was built
1. **Step 1, `port_goods`.** The outside ports sell salt and iron as they sell imports: without limit at the port, at price 1, with carriage inland as for every good. In `goods_trade` a port can be an arc's supplier for goods 1 and 2. What a market buys from a port leaves as coin with imports (`imports_out`), so `tests/money.rs` still closes.
   - At today's demand, built alone (`demand_stocks` off): hash `f0bb88df5439f2d9`. Iron from ports 36–58% of the region's iron (map 3 100%); salt 2–37% (map 3 81–86% after 1400).
2. **Step 2, `demand_stocks`.** Demand for salt, iron and stone comes from stocks, as D0 computed it (`trade::demand`). The per-head values and the D71 iron calibration are retired while it is on. Elasticities are unchanged.
   - **Salt:** `salt_lb` 15 lb a person a year at `salt_lb_per_d` 3.1 lb a penny. This is named as a calibration: Bridbury 1955 was not consulted, and no per-head figure was found.
   - **Iron:** arable km² × `ploughs_per_km2` 2 × `plough_iron_d` 36 d, plus people × `household_iron_d` 1 d, plus working sites × `site_iron_d` 24 d. The sum is divided by the value unit (43.2 d). The settlement keeps `arable_km2` (cultivation) and `works` (production) for this.
   - **Stone:** `demand_stone / stone_repair` × (`stone_repair` × W + growth of W), with `stone_repair` 0.02. Growth is W against last year's (`w_last`, set at the end of `goods_trade`).
   - With `demand_stocks` on, the ports sell salt and iron whatever `port_goods` says.
3. **One switch, `demand_stocks`,** now the default (the gate passed). Golden `65aee272c30de1f9` → **`4a9737eafc305561`**. The browser selftest (`?selftest=500`) gives the same hash at 47.5 ms a year.

## Pre-registered, reported only

   - The medians are **1,009** / 418 / 407 (p90 2,168 / 747 / 1,338). Specialists per run: 3.8 / 4.2 / 2.7.
   - The port price does cap iron's price (item 2), so a dear-iron mine boom is not the cause. Which goods map 1's large specialists work has not yet been broken down.
   - D72 had 132 / 44 / 91.
   - Step 1's precondition was confirmed in the code before the run (rule 5): `port_sells(g)` is true for g = 1, 2 whenever `demand_stocks` is on.
2. **The median town price of iron is between 1 and port parity: missed narrowly, above parity.**
   - **The data:** 10 seeds a map (`hinterlands ironprice`, `target/d1/ironprice.txt`), at 1300, 1400 and 1500. The median over markets is 1.04–1.06 on every map and year, against a median parity (1 + carriage to the nearest port) of 1.003. Every run is above parity.
   - **The excess is 4–6%.** That is the entropy margin of the price equilibrium (`market_tau`): a market buying from several suppliers pays a little over the cheapest. It is not carriage.
   - **Outliers:** 3 of 120 run-years have a median of 16–21. They are map 1 seed 4 at 1500, and map 2 seeds 9 (1400) and 3 (1500). Their cause has not been traced.
