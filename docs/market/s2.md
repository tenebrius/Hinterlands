# v6 S2: grain by equilibrium — first build (stopped)

S2 is built as approved (D77), behind `market_grain`, which is off. The golden is unchanged (`ec7a24a7caeac414`). The code is in `crates/hinterlands-core/src/systems/grain.rs`, called from trade. The run fails long before the gate: **no towns form.**

## What was built
- **The shadow decoupling** (3c87d92) and the equilibrium with budgets, rents, fees, the floor and the satiation, as in `s2-design.md` and D77. The details are in D78.
- **Two changes to the note's design, needed to make it run:**
  1. **Farmers eat their own grain first, and rent is taken in kind.** The lord's `rent_share × H` goes to market. The farmers eat from the rest at their own price and offer any surplus. Without this, a supplier at the floor sold only part of its harvest pro rata, and its own people went short of grain lying unsold at home.
  2. **The outside ports buy at most `outside_grain`, at up to a world price,** `port_world_price` 0.6, and less above it. As a fixed demand with no budget, they took 1,400 of a 1,700 harvest in year 1 and starved the region. At a world price of 1, villages eat only their need and cannot grow.
- **The solver also needed a level step:** a common shift of all prices, which leaves the flows unchanged, so that demand meets sales. Supply prices stop at the floor. With it, the grain residual is about 0.0002 in 8–11 passes, every year including the famine. Native cost is about +3 ms a year (28.9 against 25–27).

Grain sits at the floor price, and about half of every harvest is unsold. No place passes 600 people.

## Why
1. **Every place feeds itself.** Cultivation claims fields until the harvest, less rent, covers 1.2 × need. That holds for towns too, while they have land in reach. So no one needs to buy grain except the ports.
2. **The rent grain, `rent_share × H` (about 1.14 × the farmers' need at 0.49), finds no buyers,** because people stop eating at 1.2 × need. It is unsold, so the lords' rent income, and with it W's inflow, is a fraction of v5's.
3. **v5's hierarchy rests on that grain.** K = 3.39 was tuned on gross inflow that included the grain burned above `food_cap` (P3). A market person needed 3.39 units of inflow to stay. Under S2's real income, a market person can only realise what is eaten, about 1.2.

## Options for the designer
- **A. Count the market's rent at its harvest value, sold or not, towards W.** This restores v5's dynamics directly. But it brings back P3, unsold grain growing W, now as an explicit rule.
- **B. Feed market people from the market and re-tune K** (and probably ε, the growth response) for real income. This is a re-calibration of the hierarchy, which is what the D70 risk warned of. The last two rows above show it is not a small change.
- **C. (Recommended.) Make K a real grain demand.** Each market demands `K × W` grain a year for its establishment (households, servants, horses, brewing), priced like any demand and paid from its rents and fees. The satiation applies to people, not to the establishment.
  - The rent grain then has its buyers: the lords who receive the rents.
  - Nothing is burned. A market with less income than `K × W` buys less and shrinks. A famine shows at once as an establishment that cannot be kept.
  - K keeps its tuned meaning, a maintenance cost per market person, now paid in grain at market prices.
  - This is close to the proposal's option 1, W as demand. The premium m_j stays.
