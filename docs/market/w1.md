# v6 W1: local harvests (report)

Each year every cell's yield is multiplied by `m = clamp(1 + 0.15 z, 0.5, 1.5)`. `z` is a field of standard normals drawn from the run's `Rng` (Box–Muller with `libm`) on nodes 15 km apart, interpolated bilinearly to cell centres, and drawn fresh each year. Cultivation applies `m` after the fields are chosen, on top of the famine factor and before the mill bonus.

The code is in `crates/hinterlands-core/src/systems/harvest.rs`, behind `local_harvests` (off). With it off, the golden is unchanged (`ec7a24a7caeac414`). The params are in `data/real/experiments/w1.json`, and in `w1-shadow.json` with shadow prices on, for the Clark report.

## Second gate (D75): FAILED; stopped
D75 is implemented as decided:
- **Weather fixed per seed.** With `local_harvests` on, `Sim::new` draws the node field for `harvest_years` (500) years from the run's one `Rng`, before anything else draws. Later years are drawn when reached. The test `tests/harvest.rs` shows that events on and off now see the same weather.
- **sd 0.15 at every cell.** The interpolated value is divided by the interpolation's own sd at that cell, `sqrt((fx² + (1−fx)²)(fy² + (1−fy)²))`. The multiplier then has sd 0.15 at every cell (tested), rather than a uniform scale that would give 0.15 only on average.

The golden is unchanged with W1 off (`ec7a24a7caeac414`). With W1 on, map 1 seed 1 gives `f5aa4090c4ee54f1`.

- **The response is asymmetric.** A bad year sheds people and fails young places, while a good year adds only the usual growth. So variance lowers the growth rate, though not where it ends: at 0.15 the region has about 30% of its people at 1300, and 89% at 1600.
- **The famine and the plague therefore strike a much smaller, younger region.** The famine dip falls and recovery by 500 rises to 3.3–3.8.

- **Top-10 vs off stays low even with the same weather.** The events still change the random stream (founding noise, plague spread), and the hierarchy forms later and less settled.

- **Store tuning** (σ 0.10, loss 0.03, map 1): stores at year 400 stay at 0.03, and population at 200 stays at 0.58. No change.
- **Population at year 200 on map 1 seed 1, by σ:** 47,109 (0), 38,771 (0.05), 25,418 (0.10), 15,239 (0.15).

**Why the buffers do not help growth.**
- **Stores and relief act only below need.** Natural growth is `r_grow` scaled by the food ratio up to `food_cap` (1.2).
- **A village's fields give it a ratio of about 1.2 in a normal year.**
  - In a bad year, the ratio falls to 1.0–1.2: growth slows, and nothing draws on a store, because there is no deficit.
  - In a good year, the ratio rises above 1.2: the gain is capped, and the excess fills the store.
- **So weather cuts the mean growth rate** while filling stores the place never needs. Before the famine, stores are full (0.92 of need at σ 0.15), and own store meets only 3–5% of deficits.
- **The famine is buffered well:** dip 0.07–0.08. But the region it strikes is a third of the size.

## Options for the designer
- **A. Draw stores to the growth target, not to need.** In a below-par year, a place tops its food up from its store to `food_cap × need`, the level at which it grows at `r_grow`. Stores then smooth growth as well as famine. This is the direct reading of what stores were for.
- **B. Growth on a smoothed food ratio,** for example a 5-year mean, so that single bad years do not cut growth. Simpler, but it is a change to growth (D15), not to stores.
- **C. Accept lower σ.** 0.05 gives 0.82 of the dry population at 200 on one seed. But `b_Y` would be about −0.005, and the brief's floor is 0.10.

`local_harvests` stays off. The golden is unchanged (`0e65d09ec147b5c7`). Wasm was not re-timed, since the gate failed; it was 30.9 ms/year for W1 alone.
