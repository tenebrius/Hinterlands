# v6 W1: local harvests (report)

Each year every cell's yield is multiplied by `m = clamp(1 + 0.15 z, 0.5, 1.5)`. `z` is a field of standard normals drawn from the run's `Rng` (Box–Muller with `libm`) on nodes 15 km apart, interpolated bilinearly to cell centres, and drawn fresh each year. Cultivation applies `m` after the fields are chosen, on top of the famine factor and before the mill bonus.

The code is in `crates/hinterlands-core/src/systems/harvest.rs`, behind `local_harvests` (off). With it off, the golden is unchanged (`ec7a24a7caeac414`). The params are in `data/real/experiments/w1.json`, and in `w1-shadow.json` with shadow prices on, for the Clark report.

The events draw from the one `Rng` (the silting year, the plague spread), so the two runs already have different random streams. With W1 they also have different weather every year, 500 independent fields. The top ten of 1600 depends more on the weather path, so it matches less often between two weather histories.

- **Harvest CV** is that of each place's whole harvest over the years it lived (places alive 50 years or more). It includes growth, the famine and the plague, so most of it is not weather.
- **The multiplier's own CV** is about 0.095, not 0.15. Bilinear interpolation of independent nodes lowers the variance between nodes: the standard deviation of `z` falls to 0.5 at the centre of a node square.
- **The worst place in a normal year** loses about a quarter of its harvest (median multiplier 0.74). In the worst year it loses about half.
- **`b_Y` is now identified, but it is tiny,** −0.003 to −0.011, at the top of Clark's range (−0.15 to 0). Local prices still follow the regional price almost one for one (`b_P` 0.99, R² 0.98). With `market_tau` 0.002, moving grain 15 km costs about 3% of its value, so the equilibrium spreads a local shortfall over the whole region.
- **Map 2 seed 2** is the outlier, as map 2 seed 3 was under S0: `b_P` 1.35, R² 0.32.
